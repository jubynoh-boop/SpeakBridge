# Code of conduct

SpeakBridge contributors and users are expected to be respectful, constructive, and patient.

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal traits.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.
- Treat meeting audio and transcripts as sensitive data.
- Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer or GitHub's private reporting features. Enforcement decisions should prioritize community safety and may be appealed privately with relevant context.
